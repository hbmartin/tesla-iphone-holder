TESLA MODEL Y / TWO-PIECE PHONE HOLDER — V5 FIT PROTOTYPE
======================================================

V5 incorporates the fit feedback from V4. Both full inserts are prepared on
one Bambu A1 plate. V5 has not been sent to the printer. All 36 V4 output files
and archived V4 source are preserved.

Changes from V4
---------------
The outer left edge moves inward exactly 2 mm, as confirmed by the user.
Its measured wall slope is retained. The lower-left outline corner increases
from R9 to R15, and the console-facing outside edge round from R0.5 to R0.9.
These radii remain provisional pending A/B/C contour-gauge measurements.

Both nub openings measure 13.5 mm at the console-facing underside (Z=0),
including all clearance. This overrides the historical 18 mm nub-width estimate
and V4's 19.4 mm underside opening. Upper mouths remain 5.6 mm finished,
without widening chamfers or added clearance.

The left slot's upper ends move inward provisionally by 2 mm each, producing
rounded inward transitions at both ends through the thickness. Rounded capsule
ends remain in plan. Underside end positions stay unchanged. Right upper-end
reductions remain 6 mm lower / 5 mm upper versus V3, as in V4. Smoothstep
interpolation and 32 loft bands define the transitions.

Opening extents in installed Y coordinates:
  Left underside: 88.2 to 153.1 mm; upper: 90.2 to 151.1 mm.
  Right underside: 18.35 to 154.37 mm; upper: 24.35 to 149.37 mm.

Retained dimensions and seating
-------------------------------
XY follows the console pad. Y=0 is the lower factory crease; positive Y points
at the camera end. Z=0 is the console-facing surface. Nub centerlines remain
at X=0 and X=93 mm. All dimensions below are millimeters.

Phone: 71.1 x 150, centered at X=46.5, bottom at Y=0.
Camera housing: 64.5 x 41.5, centered, Y=105 to 146.5.
Camera aperture: 65.1 x 42.1, Y=104.7 to 146.8, 0.3 clearance per side.
Provisional housing R6 becomes aperture R6.3. No extra lower relief is added.

Shared outer plane: Z=1 at Y=0 to Z=4.4 at Y=160.17. Maximum thickness is
4.4, below the 4.5 hard limit. The shorter left insert reaches about 4.373.
Phone bearing plane: Z=0 at Y=0 to Z=4.1841 at Y=150. Its first 36 mm of
footprint is completely removed with 0.2 seating clearance. Above that,
contact faces are relieved to the bearing plane. One-millimeter lower rails
remain outside the phone footprint. Real phone seating remains untested.

The central 57 mm opening is retained. Twenty honeycomb openings satisfy the
left piece's web/border requirements; none qualify on the right. Remaining
material below Y=36 is solid. Other lower corners remain R9 and the right
console-facing edge round remains R0.5.

Physical test and lens diagnostic
--------------------------------
1. Remove supports from nub roofs and exterior edge rounds. The 13.5 mm
   underside mouths provide access; actual removal effort is untested.
2. Check rocking, high spots, nub-end interference and wall fit. Compare the
   narrower left side and larger lower-left radius with V4.
3. Check camera fit and whether the phone reaches the original crease. Inspect
   lens contact before pressing down; do not force the phone into place.
4. Compare stationary charging for 10 minutes with the bare-phone baseline in
   the same bay and fully seated in the crease. Record connections, dropouts
   and temperature. The supplied research favors crease seating. If the bare
   baseline is unreliable, compare a 180-degree bare-phone placement before
   attributing changes to vertical alignment. The fitted camera opening is
   designed for normal orientation.
5. Record A/B/C contour-gauge measurements when available.

The recorded 5 mm lens projection is retained. A rigid phone on an ideal flat
pad at the modeled tilt predicts about 0.91–2.07 mm lens interference across
the housing's Y range. Exact lens positions and pad compression are unmeasured.
This is a nonblocking prototype diagnostic, not verified lens clearance.
The historical scan calibrated to 72.1 mm phone width is supporting evidence;
the user's newer 71.1 mm measurement controls the design.

Validation and rebuilding
-------------------------
Checks cover one valid CAD solid and watertight mesh per insert, zero overlap,
CAD/STL volume agreement within 0.2%, the 4.4 mm maximum, taper, phone keepout,
camera aperture, 14 nub sections, the 2 mm left-edge shift, and analytic
R15/R0.9 surfaces. GUI project meshes must match the STL files. The sliced
plate has 22 layers from 0.20 to 4.40 mm, support/interface paths, and both
lower structural rails through five layers (1 mm). Traditional timelapse
notices are recorded separately from geometry/plate warnings.

In the original workspace:
  .venv/bin/python build_model.py
  .venv/bin/python make_previews.py
  .venv/bin/python prepare_slicer.py

Open output/v5/Holder_V5_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE.3mf in Bambu Studio; Slice plate.
Save Project As Holder_V5_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE_SLICED.3mf in output/v5.
Use File > Export > Export plate sliced file to save
Holder_V5_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE.gcode.3mf in the same folder. Then run:
  .venv/bin/python verify_outputs.py

The installed Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 CLI slices successfully, but its project
export path crashes. GUI export is an explicit rebuild step; verification
rejects absent or mismatched exports. Use --geometry-only to check CAD first.

The common CAD module remains model_v4.py, supporting V4 and V5 parameters.
V5 preview/verification modules are previews_v5.py and verify_v5.py. Scripts
do not dispatch prints. Archived V4 source is in revisions/v4/source/.
The package includes two V4 reference meshes and the hash manifest; the rest
of the V4 history remains in the original workspace.
