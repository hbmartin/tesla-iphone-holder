Dimensions and coordinate system
-------------------------------
XY follows the console pad; Y=0 is the factory lower crease. Positive Y points
toward the phone camera end. Z is normal to the pad, with Z=0 at the pad.
Left and right nub centerlines stay at X=0 and X=93 mm.

Phone: 71.1 x 150 mm, centered at X=46.5; bottom at Y=0.
Camera housing: 64.5 x 41.5, centered across the phone, Y=105 to146.5.
Camera aperture: 65.1 x42.1, Y=104.7 to146.8; 0.3 clearance per side.
The old extra 2 mm lower relief is removed. Housing R6 is provisional; the
clearance-offset aperture uses R6.3. These radii do not shift the datums.

Shared outer plane: Z=1.0 at Y=0 to Z=4.4 at Y=160.17.
Installed maximum is 4.4 mm, below the 4.5 mm hard limit.
The left insert ends at Y=158.9, so its maximum is about 4.373 mm.
Phone bearing plane: Z=0 at the bottom to Z=4.1841 at Y=150.
The first 36 mm of the rounded phone footprint is completely removed, with
0.2 mm seating clearance. Above it, contact faces are relieved to the bearing
plane. One-millimeter lower structural rails remain outside that footprint.
This makes room for the bottom to reach the crease; real seating is untested.

Finished upper nub mouths: exactly 5.6 mm design width, no added clearance
or widening rim chamfer. The underside mouths retain V3's 19.4 mm width:
18 mm measured base + 0.5 clearance and 0.2 legacy lead-in per side.

Left underside and upper mouth: Y=88.2 to153.1.
Right underside mouth: Y=18.35 to154.37.
Right upper mouth: Y=24.35 to149.37.
Thus the right top opening is shortened 6 mm at its bottom and 5 mm at its
top relative to V3's finished rim, without changing the underside.
Smoothstep interpolation rounds the inward transition at both heights.
Thirty-two fine loft bands avoid spline oscillations while approximating
the curve below print resolution. Width and end sections are checked in STL.
Rounded capsule ends are retained throughout the transition.

Both inserts reach Y=0. The left wall retains the measured 9 and14 mm
nub-edge-to-wall gaps and 0.5 clearance. Lower outline R9 and external
underside R0.5 remain provisional pending contour-gauge measurements A/B/C.
The central 57 mm opening remains clear. Twenty-three honeycomb holes fit
the left insert; none meet the conservative border requirement on the
narrower right insert. Remaining material below Y=36 has no honeycomb holes.

Physical test
-------------
1. Remove the supports, including those beneath the inward nub roofs.
   The 19.4 mm underside mouths provide access. Pull supports toward the
   underside before fitting either insert; removal effort is still untested.
2. Seat each insert and record rocking, high spots, end interference and
   left-wall fit. The unmeasured console and nub contours are provisional.
3. Check camera fit and whether the phone bottom reaches the original crease.
   Inspect lens contact before applying pressure. Do not force the phone down.
4. Compare stationary charging with the bare-phone baseline in the same bay,
   with the bottom fully in the crease, for 10 minutes. Record connections,
   dropouts and temperature. The supplied research favors seating at the
   crease. If the baseline is unreliable, compare a 180-degree bare-phone
   placement before drawing conclusions about vertical alignment. The fitted
   camera opening is designed for the normal orientation.
5. Record A/B/C contour-gauge measurements when available for the next revision.

Lens diagnostic
---------------
The recorded 5 mm lens projection is retained. A rigid phone on an ideal flat
pad at the modeled tilt predicts roughly 0.91–2.07 mm interference for lenses
located anywhere across the housing's Y range. Exact lens positions and pad
compression are unmeasured. This diagnostic is deliberately nonblocking for
the authorized prototype. No verified lens clearance or charging claim is made.
The old scan's 72.1 mm width calibration is supporting evidence only; the new
71.1 mm user measurement controls the model. PLA is the requested fit material.

Validation
----------
Exported STEP: valid CAD, one solid per insert, no inter-part overlap.
STL: watertight, consistent winding, one connected solid per insert.
CAD/STL volume agreement: within 0.2% at 0.025 mm mesh chord tolerance.
Independent mesh sections check mouth widths, underside preservation and
asymmetric end reductions against V3, plus camera placement and aperture size.
STEP intersections check the lower phone keepout and sloped bearing relief.

Real G-code has 22 layers from 0.20 to4.40 mm, support and interface extrusion,
and both lower rails survive through five layers (1.0 mm). GUI project meshes
match the exported STLs and report no mesh repairs. Slicer time and mass are
estimates. The GUI export includes only traditional-timelapse notices; it has
no geometry or out-of-plate warnings. The later authorized print dispatch is
recorded separately in validation/print_job.json.

Rebuild in the original workspace
--------------------------------
.venv/bin/python build_model.py
.venv/bin/python make_previews.py
.venv/bin/python prepare_slicer.py

Open output/v4/Holder_V4_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE.3mf in Bambu Studio and Slice plate.
Save Project As Holder_V4_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE_SLICED.3mf in output/v4.
Use File > Export > Export plate sliced file to save
Holder_V4_A1_PLA_PROTOTYPE.gcode.3mf in the same folder.
Then run:
.venv/bin/python verify_outputs.py

The local Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 command-line slicer produces valid G-code,
but its project-export path crashes. The GUI export above is therefore an
explicit rebuild step. Verification rejects absent or mismatched GUI exports.
Run verify_outputs.py --geometry-only to check CAD before the GUI export.

The build scripts do not send print jobs. CAD modules are model_v4.py and
build_model.py; preview modules are previews_v4.py and make_previews.py;
verification modules are verify_v4.py, slice_v4.py and verify_outputs.py.
Archived V3 source is in revisions/v3/source/. Older outputs remain in place.
The package includes V3 comparison meshes and checksums for reproducibility;
the full V3 history remains in the original workspace.
